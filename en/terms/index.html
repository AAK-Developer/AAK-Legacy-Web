Information and transparency

A clear framework for using AAK Legacy.

Principles for responsible use, availability, and the service's limits, reflecting how AAK Legacy actually works.

AAK10
01

What AAK Legacy is

An application to organize and protect, through encryption, information, instructions, and memories you decide to leave prepared for the people you choose.

02

Your responsibility as the account holder

You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of what you store, for correctly designating your guardians and recipients, and for keeping your Recovery Kit somewhere safe and separate from your devices.

03

Guardians

They help confirm when the activation process should continue. They cannot read your content or decide individually; activation requires the agreement of the threshold you configure.

04

Recipients

Each recipient receives only the content you assigned to them, never the rest of your Legacy. You decide whether they are informed now or only at the moment of delivery.

05

Activation and objections

The process includes notice periods to the account holder and, if applicable, to guardians. An account holder who reappears can stop the process at any point before final delivery.

06

Recovery Kit

Your only external recovery path. If you lose it together with every authorized device, your Legacy becomes cryptographically unrecoverable; neither AAK, nor Apple, nor support can reverse that loss.

07

Does not replace professional advice

AAK Legacy does not replace a will, a notarized instrument, or professional legal, medical, or estate advice.

08

Service availability

The service is provided with the reasonable availability typical of cloud infrastructure, without a guarantee of uninterrupted continuity. We may introduce changes required for technical, legal, or security reasons.

09

Security and the model's limits

Content is encrypted on your device before it leaves it, and AAK Legacy holds no master key to read it. This also means that, without your authorized devices or your Recovery Kit, not even AAK can help you recover the content.

10

Scope and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by Spanish law. For any dispute, the competent courts will be those applicable under the mandatory rules in force, including those protecting consumers and users.

Protect what matters. Leave it prepared.
