Plans and pricing

A Legacy that grows with you.

Before you install AAK Legacy, you can see exactly what each plan includes and how much it costs. No surprises, no small print.

AAK12

AAK Legacy

Three ways to start.

You can start for free and expand your Legacy whenever you need to. Premium and Pro are purchased and managed through the App Store.

Free

€0

No cost, no expiry

To start organizing the essentials of your Legacy.

  • Up to 10 text items
  • Up to 1 photo for each of the 5 spaces (5 photos maximum in total, never more than 1 per space)
  • 1 recipient
  • The recipient cannot be private
  • 0 guardians
  • No automatic activation
  • No custom timeframes
  • Encrypted sync and backup via CloudKit

Premium

€6.99/month

or €69.99/year

To prepare a complete Legacy with the people you trust.

  • Unlimited items
  • Attachments
  • Up to 10 recipients
  • 3 guardians · quorum 2
  • Preconfigured timeframes
  • No private recipients
  • No automatic activation
  • No custom timeframes
  • Encrypted sync and backup via CloudKit

Pro

€8.99/month

or €89.99/year

For those who need advanced control and maximum flexibility.

  • Unlimited items
  • Attachments
  • Up to 50 recipients
  • 3 guardians · quorum 2, or 5 guardians · quorum 3
  • Private recipients
  • Automatic activation
  • Custom timeframes
  • Advanced Pro features
  • Encrypted sync and backup via CloudKit

Premium and Pro are purchased and managed through the App Store. Your subscription, its renewal, plan changes and cancellation are handled directly from your App Store account, under Apple's terms.

AAK Legacy is currently in TestFlight, ahead of its public release. Once it is available on the App Store, this page will include the direct download link.
